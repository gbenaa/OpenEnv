# Disclosure rules for uk_espresso_001

The environment rewards evidence bundles that show affiliate-disclosure awareness.

For this prototype, an acceptable bundle should include at least one accepted or rejected evidence item that recognises one of the following:

1. Affiliate links must be disclosed clearly.
2. Price and availability may change.
3. Buying-guide claims need reliable product evidence rather than unsupported sales copy.
4. Commission incentives should not override user usefulness.

This is not legal advice. It is a controlled compliance signal for the environment.
