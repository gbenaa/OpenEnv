Competitor review: best budget air fryers

A competitor buying guide compares air fryers under £130.

Review themes include basket size, cleaning effort, counter-space use, and value for households of different sizes.

The guide places CrispLite 4L as best for small kitchens, FamilyCrisp 6.5L as best family budget option, and TwinCook 8L as best cheap dual-basket option.
